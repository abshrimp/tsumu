# Code of Conduct

Tsumu is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want taking part in it to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language
- Respecting different opinions, and giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, including about anyone's identity or appearance
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or personal and political attacks
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an email or physical address, without their permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@abshrimp](https://github.com/abshrimp). If a report cannot be made publicly, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without including the details. Reports are handled promptly and as confidentially as possible.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community. Depending on the severity, the response ranges from a private warning to a permanent ban.
