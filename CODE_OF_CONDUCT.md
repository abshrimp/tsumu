# Code of Conduct

Tsumu is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want taking part to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language
- Respecting different opinions, and giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, including about anyone's identity or appearance
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or personal and political attacks
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an email or physical address, without their permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@abshrimp](https://github.com/abshrimp). If you can't make the report publicly, open an issue asking for a private contact channel, without including any details. Reports are handled promptly and as confidentially as possible.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code. Depending on how serious the behavior is, the response ranges from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban for anyone whose behavior harms the community.
